## ReelKit's Unique Position

### ✅ Strengths
1. **Single-purpose focus** — Does ONE thing exceptionally well
2. **Cost** — Free self-hosted, cheap cloud vs $10-60/mo competitors
3. **Speed** — 30 seconds vs minutes in competing tools
4. **Open source** — Community-driven, transparent
5. **Platform-specific** — Optimized per social platform
6. **No learning curve** — Drag-drop simplicity
7. **Creator-aligned** — Built by creators, for creators
8. **Self-hosted option** — Own your data

### ❌ Weaknesses (vs. competitors)
1. No effects/transitions
2. No stock footage
3. No video creation (only repurposing)
4. Limited analytics
5. No direct publishing (yet)
